# Black Box

## Register
Brand: a marketing homepage for Black Box, an AI agent flight recorder.

## Users and purpose
Teams shipping AI agents, security reviewers, and insurers need evidence of recorded actions that can be checked independently. The homepage's sole conversion is a 15-minute private briefing through the existing Calendly URL.

## Voice and principles
Cryptic, precise, forensic. Say tamper-evident, never tamper-proof. No fabricated customers, metrics, testimonials, or pricing on the homepage. Essential claims stay readable without JavaScript or motion.

## Direction
A short cinematic experience inspired by fantasy.co's visual scale and bynar.io's security atmosphere. One persistent higher-dimensional object illustrates record, verify, and reconstruct. Ordinary scrolling, no pinned narrative. Existing educational and legal routes remain available.
